Product Thinking

How I think about AI products.

I believe successful AI products are not defined by model accuracy alone—they succeed when they solve meaningful user problems, fit naturally into existing workflows, and create measurable business impact.

When designing AI systems, I focus on user value, evaluation, adoption, trust, latency, cost, and responsible human oversight alongside technical performance.
